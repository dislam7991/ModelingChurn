4 · The 20% discount — economics

Offering trades full margin for 80% of margin but guarantees retention, so EV(offer) − EV(no offer) = margin × (p − 0.20). Offer a positive-margin customer only when churn probability p > 0.20.

5 · Recommendation

Do not offer the 20% discount broadly — it destroys ~224,983/yr because most customers would not have churned. Target the ~824 customers the model scores above 20% churn probability and who carry positive margin; rank them by expected value margin × (p − 0.20). This retains ~260 real churners and beats the blanket offer by ~248,302/yr. The scored verification set is in outputs/predictions/ml_case_test_output_filled.csv.

Model AUC ~0.70: use the ranking plus the margin filter, not individual raw labels. Dollar figures assume every offered customer accepts and stays a year (per the brief) and use net_margin as the annual margin proxy.
