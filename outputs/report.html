Calibration — a defect fixed

v2 used isotonic calibration, whose step function tied customers together. Calibration was chosen by cross-fitted profit on training data only: sigmoid 29,615 vs isotonic 28,856 (training-fold uplift). On the holdout the old isotonic version earns 2,862/yr more — the two sources disagree, both gaps are small, and the rule was fixed before the holdout was seen, so it stands. Sigmoid keeps every customer's score distinct (4,024 vs 48 under isotonic), so the ranked list the brief asks for is exact (holdout PR-AUC 0.360 vs 0.341).

9 · Recommendation

Do not offer the 20% discount broadly — a blanket offer loses money. Use XGB v2 (sigmoid calibration) and offer the discount to the ~1,268 customers above the profit threshold (0.21) with positive margin, ranked by margin × (p − 0.20). If all accept, that retains ~528 churners (churn 9.9% → 6.6%) and adds ~64,259/yr. If only half of would-be churners accept while every stayer does, uplift falls to ~14,842/yr — so launch it as a randomised pilot and collect the roadmap data before scaling. V3 Tier-1 tests found no significant gain, so XGB v2 stays; its calibration moved to sigmoid.

Model ROC-AUC ~0.71: use the ranking plus the margin filter, not individual raw labels. Headline dollar figures follow the brief's assumption that every offered customer accepts and stays a year (see the acceptance sensitivity for what happens when they don't) and use net_margin as the annual margin proxy.
